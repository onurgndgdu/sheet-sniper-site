Installing it

Sheet Sniper is not sold through the Chrome Web Store, so installation is a manual, one-time process — and worth knowing about before you buy.

You unzip the download into a folder you keep, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click "Load unpacked" and select that folder. Then you paste your licence key into the extension. Written instructions come with the download.

Two consequences. Chrome shows a "Disable developer mode extensions" notice on some startups — you dismiss it and carry on, but it keeps coming back. And there is no auto-update: new versions arrive by email and you repeat the unzip step.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium-based browsers.

Privacy

We have no servers. Finding the table, reading it and cleaning it all happen inside your browser. The contents of the pages you visit never reach us.

Choose CSV and the data never leaves your computer at all. Choose Google Sheets and the table goes straight from your browser into your own Google account, using a permission that only lets the extension touch files it created itself.
