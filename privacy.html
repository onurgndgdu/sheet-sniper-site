Privacy Policy

Sheet Sniper · Last updated 21 September 2026

Sheet Sniper has no servers. Everything it does — finding tables, reading them, cleaning numbers and dates — happens inside your browser. We never receive the contents of the pages you visit, and we do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension reads

When you click the Sheet Sniper icon and choose Select table, the extension reads the visible contents of the tab you are on so it can find tables and extract the one you pick. It does this only on the tab that is active when you click, and only after you click. It does not run in the background, does not read other tabs, and does not watch your browsing.

This is why the extension asks for activeTab and scripting rather than permission for all websites: it has no access to a page until you explicitly invoke it there.

Where your data goes

It depends on the export you choose, and nothing happens without your choice.

Export Where the table goes
CSV download Nowhere. The file is generated in your browser and saved to your computer. No network request is made.
Google Sheets Into your own Google account. The table is sent directly from your browser to Google's Sheets API. It does not pass through us.

Google account access

If you choose the Google Sheets export, you are asked to sign in to Google. Sheet Sniper requests a single, narrow permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

This scope lets the extension create new spreadsheets and write to the files it created. It does not give access to your existing Google Drive files, and the extension cannot read, modify or delete anything it did not make itself. The access token is handled by Chrome and is never sent to us.

You can revoke this access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sheet Sniper's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored on your device

The extension keeps a small amount of information in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • your export preference (CSV or Google Sheets);
  • your licence key and the result of the last check against it.

Payments

Sheet Sniper is sold through Gumroad, which acts as the seller of record. When you buy, your email address and payment details are handled by Gumroad and governed by their privacy policy. We never see or store your card details.

Inside the extension, the only thing stored is the licence key you paste in, and the only request made to Gumroad is to check that the key is valid and has not been refunded. That request contains the key and the product id, nothing else, and it is made at most once a day.

No account is created and no personal data is collected by the extension itself.

Analytics and tracking

There are none. No analytics service, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, no cookies set by us, and no profile of your browsing.

Children

Sheet Sniper is a productivity tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects how data is handled, the updated date at the top of this page will change and the new version will be published here before the change takes effect in a released version of the extension.
